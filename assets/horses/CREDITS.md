# Horse photograph credits

The six photographs below are real photographs, not AI-generated images. They are used as representative images for fictional demonstration horse profiles; the photographers and real photographed horses are not affiliated with STRIDE. Profile names, breeds, ownership and health data in the prototype are fictional and do not identify or describe the actual photographed horses.

Downloaded and source/license details checked on 28 October 2026. Local copies have been resized, JPEG-compressed and stripped of embedded metadata for a lightweight offline prototype. No AI generation or compositing was used. The dashboard may reuse aurora.jpg.
